From the ad click to the clinic chair. With the truth coming back.

Ybyatã is an agency and a software company for physiotherapy and health clinics. We run the clinics' ads on Google Ads and Meta, and our platform follows every patient they bring: from the click, through the conversation on the front desk's WhatsApp, to the appointment booked and attended. What happens at the clinic goes back to the ad as a conversion. That's how the spend learns from reality.

How it works

  1. The ad brings the patient

    Campaigns on Google Ads and Meta lead to the clinic's page. The page keeps the click identifier (Google's GCLID, Meta's click id) together with the contact the person leaves. Every lead is born knowing where it came from.

  2. The conversation happens on the clinic's WhatsApp

    The front desk answers as it always has, on WhatsApp Business. The platform follows the conversation through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta) and turns it into signals: an appointment booked, a patient who came, a price objection, a question about insurance. No spreadsheets, and with privacy taken seriously.

  3. The truth goes back to the ad

    When the lead becomes a booked or attended appointment, the platform sends that conversion back to the ad platform that brought it: offline conversions in Google Ads (by GCLID) and conversion events in Meta. Phone and e-mail travel only as SHA-256 hashes; nothing else about the patient leaves the system.

  4. The dashboard shows the cost of what matters

    For each campaign: spend, leads, appointments booked and attended, and the cost per booking. Ybyatã's team operates the accounts; the clinic's owner sees their own clinic's report, readable in a minute.

Google Ads at Ybyatã

The model

The clinics' Google Ads accounts are linked to Ybyatã's manager account (MCC), and Ybyatã's team operates them. The Google Ads API integration uses Ybyatã's own credentials, runs in server jobs and is never called by an end user; no one at the clinic signs in to Google through the platform.

What the integration does

  • Reporting: every six hours, one campaign-level query per account re-reads the daily spend of the last seven days, for the cost-per-booking dashboard.
  • Offline conversions: bookings and visits go back to Google by the click id (GCLID); without one, as enhanced conversions for leads (phone and e-mail only as hashes). Every upload is idempotent.
  • That's all: the API doesn't create, change or remove campaigns, ads, budgets or bids. Ybyatã's team manages campaigns in the Google Ads interface.

Where we are

The platform is built and runs on Google Cloud, in São Paulo, with the first client clinic being onboarded. The full design of the integration is published in Google Ads API tool design. The tool is for Ybyatã and the clinics it serves; it is not sold or licensed to third parties.

Privacy

Each clinic is its own space, isolated from the others by the database itself. Everything stays in São Paulo, encrypted in transit and at rest; the credentials a clinic connects (WhatsApp, payments) are also encrypted by the application, with the key kept outside the database. What goes to Google Ads and Meta is the conversion and a hash of phone and e-mail, never the conversation. The clinic is the controller of its patients' data; Ybyatã is the processor, under Brazil's LGPD.
